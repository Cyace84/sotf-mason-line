[size=5][b]Mason Line[/b][/size]

A stake-and-rope guide line you craft from 1 stick and 1 rope. Plant a stake where a wall should start, plant another where it should end, and a cord pulls tight between them. Any log you set down within 2 meters of the cord snaps onto it, so the wall runs stake to stake instead of wherever the first log happened to point.

That last part is the actual problem. Sons of the Forest stacks logs perfectly: every log after the first snaps onto the one before it. But the direction comes from the first log, dropped by hand with nothing to line it up against. A degree off there and ten logs later the wall is level, solid, and half a meter to the left of the rock you were building toward. The angle cannot be nudged afterwards. You tear it down and try to eyeball it better.

The sane fix is to place that first log more carefully. I wrote a Harmony patch instead. Most of my mods are this size.

[size=4][b]Controls[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Left-click while holding the Mason Line plants a stake at the crosshair. First click = stake A, second = stake B, cord runs between them.
[*]Hold Dismantle while looking at a stake to collect the line. The kit returns to your inventory. That is the vanilla action under Construction in the controls menu, bound to C by default.
[/list]
No custom keybinds. The mod adds no hotkeys of its own and reads your existing bindings: left-click places, the Dismantle action collects. Rebind Dismantle and this follows it.

[size=4][b]Multiple lines[/b][/size]
Each crafted kit is one line. Craft more for parallel lines or corners. They all snap at the same time, and a log goes to whichever cord is closest.

[size=4][b]What it does under the hood[/b][/size]
One Harmony postfix on [font=Courier New]TargetInfo.CalcRelativePlacePosition[/font]. When a free log's placement lands within 2 meters of a cord and between the two stakes, its position and axis get projected onto the line. The build preview, the green/red validation and the log that finally drops all read that one field. There is no grid involved, and nothing registers a snap node.

The mod also patches [font=Courier New]ObjectPhysicsInteractionSfx.TryTriggerHardSurfaceImpact[/font] as a crash guard for a vanilla bug where [font=Courier New]Physics.ClosestPoint[/font] hits a non-convex mesh collider in the backpack physics callback and kills the process. That patch is game-wide, not specific to the guide line.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://github.com/ToniMacaroni/RedLoader]RedLoader[/url] and run the game once.
[*]Unpack the archive into the game's [font=Courier New]Mods/[/font] folder. You should end up with [font=Courier New]Mods/MasonLine.dll[/font] and a [font=Courier New]Mods/MasonLine/[/font] folder holding the manifest.
[*]Start the game. The recipe shows up on the crafting mat as Mason Line.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Notes[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Tested in singleplayer. Multiplayer is untested. Reports welcome.
[*]The cord is a visual guide, not a physical object. NPCs and the player walk through it.
[*]Saving and loading preserves your kit inventory. Lines already stretched on the ground are session-only. Collect them and re-stretch after loading.
[/list]
