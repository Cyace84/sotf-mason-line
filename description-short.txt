A stake-and-rope guide line you craft from 2 sticks and 1 rope. Plant a stake where a wall should start, plant another where it should end, and a cord pulls tight between them. Set a log down beside the cord and it snaps onto it, so the wall runs stake to stake instead of wherever the first log happened to point.

That last part is the actual problem. The game stacks logs perfectly: every log after the first snaps onto the one before it. But the direction comes from the first log, dropped by hand with nothing to line it up against. A degree off there and ten logs later the wall is level, solid, and half a meter to the left of the rock you were building toward. The angle cannot be nudged afterwards.

The sane fix is to place that first log more carefully. I wrote a Harmony patch instead.

**Controls.** No custom keybinds. Left-click plants a stake: first click is A, second is B. Hold Dismantle (C by default) on a stake to collect the line; the bundle returns to your pack.

**What snaps.** More than logs: logs flat or standing, standing sticks, campfires, rocks — anything the game lets you set down loose on open ground. Guidebook blueprints, the ghosts you fill in piece by piece, do not.

**Multiple lines.** Each bundle is one line, all working at once; a log goes to the closest cord. Aim a new line's first stake at one already standing and it lands exactly on it, so a run can turn a corner and carry on from the same point. Nothing squares up the angle between them, though, so that part is yours to judge.

**Settings.** Snap distance (0.3 m), reach past the stakes (0.1 m) and stake magnet (0.3 m), in the mod menu, applied the moment you change them.

**Notes.** Lines are not part of the save: reload and the line is gone, while its bundle is back in your pack. The cord is a guide, not an object, so everything walks through it. Co-op works peer-to-peer, one line per player.
