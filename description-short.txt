A stake-and-rope guide line you craft from 2 sticks and 1 rope. Plant a stake where a wall should start, another where it should end, and a cord pulls tight between them. Set a log down beside it and it snaps onto the line, so the wall runs stake to stake instead of wherever the first log happened to point.

The game stacks logs perfectly, but the direction comes from the first one, dropped by hand with nothing to line it up against. A degree off there and ten logs later the wall is solid, level, and half a meter from where you meant it to be.

**Controls.** No custom keybinds. Left-click plants a stake, first A then B. Hold Dismantle (C by default) on a stake to collect the line and get the bundle back.

**What snaps.** Logs flat or standing, standing sticks, campfires, rocks — anything you set down loose on open ground. Guidebook blueprints do not.

**Notes.** Each bundle is one line and they all work at once. Lines are not part of the save: reload and the line is gone, its bundle back in your pack. Co-op works peer-to-peer, one line per player.
